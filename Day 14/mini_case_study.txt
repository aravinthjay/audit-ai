Step 1: Ingest incoming customer emails and use an LLM-based classification chain to categorize them (billing, technical issue, refund request, account access, etc.).

Step 2: Store company policies and knowledge-base documents in a vector database and perform RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) to retrieve the most relevant documents.

Step 3: Generate a draft response using the retrieved policy documents and the customer email context.

Step 4: If confidence is low or the issue is classified as complex, trigger a tool/API call to create a ticket and escalate the case to a human support agent.

Justification:
LangGraph is ideal for orchestrating multi-step workflows with decision points (classify → retrieve → generate → escalate). LangChain provides integrations with LLMs, vector databases, retrievers, and external APIs. Together they support scalable, agentic customer-support automation while maintaining human-in-the-loop escalation for complex cases.

High-Level Architecture
1.Email Inbox → Email Processing Service
2.LangGraph Workflow starts
3.LLM Classifier categorizes email
4.RAG retrieves relevant policy documents from Vector DB
5.LLM generates draft response
Confidence Check → Send response OR create ticket via Ticketing API and route to human agent
