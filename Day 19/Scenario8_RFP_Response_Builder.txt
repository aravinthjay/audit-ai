Scenario 8 of 8 – RFP Response Builder

Correct Pattern: GraphFlow


Scenario:
A bid response has four sections:
- Technical
- Pricing
- Compliance
- Timeline

Each section is owned by a specialist and assembled in order.
A reviewer then checks the assembled draft and may send specific sections
back for rework before final sign-off.

Why GraphFlow?

This workflow is structured, predictable, and contains explicit review loops.

Key characteristics:
1. Known sequence of work.
2. Multiple specialist agents responsible for predefined sections.
3. Assembly step combines all sections.
4. Reviewer performs quality checks.
5. Conditional routing sends specific sections back for rework.
6. Final approval ends the workflow.

GraphFlow is designed for:
- Deterministic workflows
- Conditional branches
- Review and approval loops
- Structured orchestration between agents

Why not the others?

Round-robin:
- Merely cycles through agents.
- Does not naturally support targeted rework loops.

Selector:
- Useful when choosing the next agent dynamically.
- Here the workflow is already known.

Swarm / Handoff:
- Best when agents decide who should act next.
- This process follows a predefined structure.

Magentic:
- Strong runner-up because specialists collaborate on a shared document.
- However, the overall process is governed by a fixed workflow, making GraphFlow the better fit.
