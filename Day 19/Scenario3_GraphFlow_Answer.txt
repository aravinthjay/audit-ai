Scenario 3 of 8 – Editorial Workflow

Correct Pattern: GraphFlow

Reason:
A newsroom drafts a short article with a fixed three-stage loop:
1. Writer drafts
2. Editor critiques
3. Writer revises

The same roles execute in the same order repeatedly until the editor approves the article.

Why GraphFlow?
- Fixed sequence of agents
- Predefined workflow
- Conditional looping until approval
- Deterministic process

Why not the others?
- Round-robin: Cycles through agents but does not naturally model approval-based workflow.
- Selector: Used when a controller dynamically chooses the next agent.
- Swarm/Handoff: Agents decide who takes over next.
- Magentic: Designed for more dynamic collaborative interactions.

Workflow:

Writer
   ↓
Editor
   ↓
Approved?
 ├─ Yes → End
 └─ No  → Writer (revise)

Answer: GraphFlow
