Scenario 7 of 8 – Buyer's Research Assistant

Correct Pattern: Selector



Reason:
A merchandising team asks:
"Find three trending materials for outdoor furniture this season and summarize supplier options."

The number and type of subtasks are not known in advance and may require:
- Web searches
- Supplier lookups
- Market trend analysis
- Data gathering from multiple sources

Why Selector?
- A central coordinator decides which specialist agent should be used next.
- The workflow is dynamic, but task routing remains controlled.
- Different agents can be selected depending on what information is needed.
- The final response is assembled from the outputs of the selected specialists.

Why not the others?
- Round-robin: Unnecessary fixed rotation.
- GraphFlow: The exact workflow is not known beforehand.
- Swarm / Handoff: Could work, but agents do not need to autonomously transfer control.
- Magentic: Collaboration is possible, but explicit task selection is the primary need.

Example Flow:

User Request
      ↓
Selector Agent
      ↓
Trend Research Agent
      ↓
Supplier Lookup Agent
      ↓
Market Analysis Agent
      ↓
Final Summary

Why this is a Close Call:
Swarm / Handoff is a strong runner-up because the investigation may evolve as new findings emerge. However, the scenario is best modeled by a coordinator choosing the most appropriate specialist at each step rather than agents handing off control themselves.

Answer: Selector
Runner-up: Swarm / Handoff
