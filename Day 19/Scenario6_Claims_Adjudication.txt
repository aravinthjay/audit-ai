Scenario 6 of 8 – Claims Adjudication

Correct Pattern: GraphFlow


Reason:
A claim requires three independent checks:
1. Fraud screening
2. Policy coverage verification
3. Medical coding review

These checks can run in parallel. A final decision agent then combines the results and determines whether to approve or deny the claim.

Why GraphFlow?
- Predefined workflow
- Parallel execution branches
- Synchronization of results
- Final aggregation and decision step
- Deterministic process with known stages

Why not the others?
- Round-robin: Not a sequential agent conversation.
- Selector: All three checks are required, so selecting one agent at a time is unnecessary.
- Swarm / Handoff: No dynamic routing is needed because the workflow is known in advance.
- Magentic: Collaboration is possible, but GraphFlow better represents structured orchestration.

Example Workflow:

Claim Submitted
       │
 ┌─────┼─────┐
 │     │     │
 ▼     ▼     ▼
Fraud Policy Medical
Check Coverage Coding
 │     │     │
 └─────┼─────┘
       ▼
Decision Agent
       ▼
Approve / Deny

Why this is a Close Call:
Magentic is a reasonable runner-up because multiple specialists contribute information that is later synthesized. However, the workflow itself is fixed and structured, making GraphFlow the stronger choice.

Answer: GraphFlow
Runner-up: Magentic
