APOLLO DIAGNOSTICS: AUTOMATED RADIOLOGY REPORT + CARE PATHWAY
============================================================

1. ARCHITECTURAL CHOICE: MULTI-AGENT SYSTEM
-------------------------------------------
Based on the case constraints, a Multi-Agent architecture is the correct choice due to:
- 4 Distinct Domains: Requires separate specialist scopes (Radiology, Clinical Safety, EMR Logistics, Communication).
- Sequential with Gates: Requires explicit failure isolation and validation checks between steps.
- Different Tool Access: Limits tool blast radius (e.g., only the Scheduling Agent accesses the EMR API).

3. PIPELINE STAGE BREAKDOWN
---------------------------
- Stage 1: Focuses on visual pattern recognition and clinical finding extraction.
- Stage 2: Operates as a logical safety filter mapping findings against history.
- Stage 3: Executes structured transactional operations with the external EMR system.
- Stage 4: Handles personalization, empathy framing, and translation of complex jargon into layman terms.
