CONTRACTIQ: M&A DUE DILIGENCE ON 800 CONTRACTS
================================================

1. ARCHITECTURAL CHOICE: MULTI-AGENT SYSTEM
-------------------------------------------
Based on the case constraints, a Multi-Agent architecture is the correct choice due to:
- Distinct Separation of Stages: The workflow transitions from high-volume parallel independent tasks (data extraction) to highly interconnected, global synthesis (identifying cascading cross-contract risks).
- Inter-contract Dependencies: A single agent cannot efficiently cross-reference and track deep systemic connections (like cascading change-of-control clauses) across 800 documents without experiencing catastrophic context overflow or hallucination.
- Specialized Capabilities Needed: The workload requires independent legal extraction "workers" feeding data into a specialized "Synthesizer/Risk Profiler" agent capable of evaluating macro regulatory compliance and assembling a dynamic RAG / Heat Map summary.

3. PIPELINE STAGE BREAKDOWN
---------------------------
- Stage 1 (Extraction Workers): Swarm of fast, narrow-prompted agents running in parallel to maximize throughput and meet the 4-hour SLA.
- Stage 2 (Synthesis Experts): Specialized analytical agents that evaluate the *collective* body of structured data rather than text. 
- Stage 3 (Visual Summary): Aggregates the risk graph and compliance gaps into the definitive legal deliverable.
